5. The diagnosis component present in source is not connected to the `/diagnose` route. Its scoring logic differs from the original Java rule table, and the known combined-result case is not demonstrated as fixed.
6. The website has no Tagalog content toggle or translation fallback. Its inspected content is English-only.
